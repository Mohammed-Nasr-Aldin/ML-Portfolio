A credit score prediction system built on 100,000 banking customer profiles.

Credit Score Classification

Predicts a customer's credit category (Good, Standard, or Poor) from engineered financial ratios, using an optimized ensemble classifier.

  • 76% test accuracy with Random Forest
  • 9 high-impact features selected from 18 raw inputs
  • Engineered ratios: Debt-to-Income and EMI
